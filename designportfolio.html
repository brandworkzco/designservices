About me

Design with a point of view.

I build brands that feel like people: distinctive, expressive and easy to remember. My work brings strategy and personality together, turning big ideas into visual systems that can live everywhere.

I spent five years of my professional life helping clients find their visual identities and bring them to light in the best way possible.
